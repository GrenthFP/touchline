Touchline: Football Management

Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Touchline never asks for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your careers, settings and any databases you import stay on your phone.
  • The game uses three Google services: Firebase Analytics (usage statistics), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and AdMob (ads). They receive device information and identifiers, described below.
  • You can turn off usage statistics and crash reports in the game, and change your ad choices at any time.

Who we are

This policy covers the Android app Touchline: Football Management (“Touchline”, “the game”, “we”), a single-player football management game made by an independent developer. Questions about it go to the contact address at the end of this page.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the game is stored only on your phone and is never uploaded by us:

  • your saved careers, including your manager name, club, squad and match history;
  • databases you import (spreadsheets, CSV or ZIP files) and the club badges inside them;
  • your settings.

When you use Export database, the game writes a spreadsheet to the place you choose. What happens to it after that is up to you. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data.

What is collected, and why

Usage statistics Firebase Analytics

To see which parts of the game people enjoy and where they get stuck, the game sends anonymous usage events: the screens you open and game milestones such as starting a career, finishing a match or ending a season. Event details are numbers and short codes (for example a league position or a match speed), never names you typed. Firebase also records an app instance identifier, your device model, Android version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your IP address. Collection of the advertising ID by Analytics is switched off.

Crash reports Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, a report is sent so the problem can be fixed: the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, available memory, an installation identifier and the last screens you visited.

You can turn off both usage statistics and crash reports in Club → Settings → Share crash reports and usage stats. Nothing more is sent after that.

Ads Google AdMob

Touchline is free and shows occasional ads: an ad between matches at most once every 15 minutes of play, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for a free opposition report. Google AdMob, which serves the ads, collects your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which it estimates an approximate location), device information and how you interact with ads. It uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads can be personalised. You can change your choice in Club → Settings → Privacy and ad choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings under Google → Ads. How Google uses this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What is not collected

Touchline does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or payment details, and it has no chat or other way to talk to other players.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google, which runs Firebase and AdMob for us under its own terms: the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. All of it is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which Google deletes them. Ad data is kept according to Google’s policies.

Children

Touchline is meant for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their data removed, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Because Touchline has no accounts, the data held by Firebase is tied only to a random identifier on your phone. The quickest way to stop collection is the Settings switch above; uninstalling the game removes the identifier. For any other request, write to the address below and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
